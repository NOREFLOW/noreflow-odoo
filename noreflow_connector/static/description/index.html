Noreflow for Odoo

An AI assistant on WhatsApp, with every sale written to your Odoo

Noreflow is a customer messaging platform for businesses in the Gulf. Its assistant, Nori, answers your customers on WhatsApp in Arabic and English, books appointments and takes orders, and hands the conversation to your team when needed.

With this module, every sale, invoice and receipt issued in Noreflow is written to this Odoo as a posted customer invoice, with the Noreflow number as its reference, and the payment is registered against it. A document is never sent twice.

How to connect

  1. Install the module, then open Accounting, Configuration, Noreflow.
  2. Create an API key in Odoo (your profile, Account Security, New API Key).
  3. Press "Open Noreflow", sign in or create an account, and paste the key. Your Odoo address and database are filled in.

What Noreflow reads and writes

Noreflow creates customers, customer invoices and payments in your Odoo for the sales made in Noreflow. It does not change or delete anything else. Delete the API key in Odoo to stop the connection at any time.
